Actuallist Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

Actuallist ("the app") helps you plan goals and keep lists. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The app is published by Varun Mohandas ("I", "me").

Information the app collects

  • Google account information. When you sign in with Google, the app receives your name, email address, profile photo and a unique account ID through Firebase Authentication. This is used to sign you in and to keep your data tied to your account.
  • Content you create. Your goals, milestones, tasks, lists, items and saved links (including links you share into the app) are stored in Cloud Firestore under your account so they are available when you sign in again.
  • Crash and diagnostic data. The app uses Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports and error logs. These include technical details such as device model, operating system version, app version and the stack trace of the error, along with an anonymous installation identifier.

How the information is used

Your information is used only to provide the app (sign-in and keeping your plans), to keep it reliable (fixing crashes), and to respond to your requests. I do not sell your personal information, do not use it for advertising, and do not share it with third parties except the service providers below.

Service providers

The app relies on Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Crashlytics) and Google Sign-In. Google processes data on my behalf under its own terms and privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Content you copy into other tools

The app can generate a prompt that you may copy into an AI assistant of your choice and paste the reply back. The app does not send that text to any AI service itself. What you share with such a tool is governed by that tool's own policy.

Purchases

Actuallist offers an optional paid upgrade, "Plus", as a monthly or yearly subscription or a one-time purchase. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details, card number or billing address. The app asks Google Play whether your Google account has an active Plus purchase and keeps the result on your device, so Plus still works offline for a short time. We do not link purchases to your Actuallist account or store them on our servers. Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions. Google's handling of payment data is described in the Google Play and Google Payments privacy notices.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account is active. To have your account and all the data stored for it deleted, email varundasm94@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, and I will delete it within 30 days. Crash reports are retained by Firebase Crashlytics for a limited period (currently 90 days) and are not linked to your name or email.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored with access rules so that each signed-in user can only read and write their own data. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so I cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information, contact me and I will delete it.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it last changed, and material changes will be reflected here.
